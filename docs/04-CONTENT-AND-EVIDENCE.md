## DO NOT create these claims
- “Raised engagement by 200%”, “100+ projects”, “served 10 clients”, “award-winning designer”, “available for hire” or other unsupported statistics/status.
- “Full-stack developer” simply because user practiced vibe coding.
- “Official winner” when user stated finalist.
- “Managed strategy/analytics/ads” unless user confirms actual responsibilities.
- Business website published to production until verified.
- Copyright ownership or permission over institutional creative assets without checking.
